The Conversational Challenge

Why Text-Only Sentiment Fails in Real Dialogues

Contact center calls and real-world audio conversations contain acoustic subtleties that break naive NLP pipelines.

⚠️
Traditional Naive Pipeline Flaws
  • Pre-chunking before Whisper: Slicing audio into 2s segments destroys Whisper's 30-second context, creating hallucinated text loops and clipped syllables.
  • Blind Overlap Erasure: Treating multi-speaker cross-talk as noise discards the most important signal of caller frustration: interruptions and over-talk.
  • Isolated Sentence Scoring: Classifying "Oh wonderful, another fee" without previous turns flags it as HAPPY (100% false positive sarcasm failure).
  • Acoustic False Alarms: Polite questions ("Would that work for you?") end on rising pitch inflection, frequently misclassified as ANGRY without question normalization.
✨
Our Multimodal Intelligence Solution
  • Continuous Global Transcription: Transcribe the full stream first with word timestamps; segment downstream along speaker turns & linguistic bounds.
  • Overlap As Conversational Intelligence: Native Pyannote overlap tracking records is_interruption, interrupted_by, and overtalk_ratio.
  • Batched Context Dialogue LLM: Sends the full conversation chronology to Qwen 2.5 72B in one single request to comprehend the entire narrative arc.
  • Bimodal Incongruence Engine: Compares semantic sentiment against acoustic tone to expose hidden hostility, sarcasm, and nervous customer masking.
💡 Key Behavioral Principle

When acoustic tone says ANGRY / TENSE while semantic words say "Thank you so much", our platform does not average them into NEUTRAL. It flags an explicit Masked Incongruence Anomaly (Hostile Sarcasm) and escalates to supervisory review.

⚡
FastAPI Lifespan & Thread Offloading
  • Lifespan Context Manager: Models are instantiated once at application startup and cached in app.state.orchestrator, avoiding re-loading overhead per request.
  • Async Worker Threadpool: Heavy synchronous CUDA pipelines run inside asyncio.to_thread(self._process_synchronous, file_path), preventing blocking of the async event loop.
  • Single-Roundtrip Cloud Batching: All 40+ turns of a conversation are batched into one single HTTP request to OpenRouter, returning in ~1.5s rather than 40 sequential roundtrips.
🧩
Hexagonal Factory Pattern
  • Central Factory (core/factory.py): Reads .env variables (WHISPER_MODEL, SEMANTIC_ENGINE, DEVICE) and instantiates the matching adapter.
  • Zero-Cloud Fallback: If OPENROUTER_API_KEY is absent, gracefully switches to local DistilRoBERTa semantic classification.
  • Instant Mockability: In-memory mock adapters (MockASR, MockDiarizer, MockSemanticScorer) enable instant unit testing without GPU weights.
Software Design Pattern

Hexagonal (Ports & Adapters) Architecture

Decoupling hardware-bound ML models from domain business logic ensures effortless testing, mockability, and zero vendor lock-in.

🔌

  • BaseASR: transcribe(file_path, beam_size, vad_filter) -> List[RawSegment]
  • BaseDiarizer: diarize_with_overlaps(file_path) -> (SpeakerIntervals, OverlapIntervals)
  • BaseSemanticScorer: score_dialogue_turns(turns) -> List[EmotionPrediction]
  • BaseAcousticScorer: score_audio_slices(file_path, segments) -> List[EmotionPrediction]
🧩
Pluggable Concrete Adapters

Easily swapped via .env settings through the central app/core/factory.py:

  • ASR Adapters: FasterWhisperASRAdapter | MockASRAdapter
  • Diarizer Adapters: PyannoteDiarizerAdapter | MockDiarizerAdapter
  • Semantic Adapters: QwenSemanticAdapter (OpenRouter) | DistilRoBERTaAdapter | MockSemanticScorer
  • Acoustic Adapters: Emotion2VecAcousticAdapter | MockAcousticScorer

⚡
Engineering Sizing Decisions
  • Why Cloud Qwen 72B over Local 7B?
    A local 7B model would take 5-6 GB VRAM and struggle with subtle corporate sarcasm. Offloading to Qwen 72B via OpenRouter costs <$0.001 per call, uses 0 GB local VRAM, and evaluates 40 dialogue turns in a single 1.5s network request.
  • CTranslate2 vs PyTorch Whisper:
    CTranslate2 provides custom kernel execution for transformer attention, reducing Whisper's VRAM requirement by 60% and inference latency by 4x.
  • Lifespan Context Manager:
    FastAPI's @asynccontextmanager initializes models exactly once during application boot, avoiding re-instantiation overhead on subsequent HTTP requests.
Core Technical Innovations

Algorithmic Engineering in the Alignment Layer

Raw model outputs are noisy. Our domain services transform raw timestamps into coherent conversational intelligence.

🛡️
1. Honorific & Boundary Protection

Standard NLP sentence splitters break upon seeing a period. Our AlignmentService explicitly protects abbreviations:

// Protected Honorifics Regex:
r'\b(Mr|Mrs|Ms|Dr|Prof|Sr|Jr)\.\s*$'
// Prevents "Hello Dr. Smith" from prematurely cutting the turn.
  • Natural Pause Splitting: Silences $\ge 1.2\text{s}$ cleanly mark turn breaks.
  • Punctuation Boundary Healing: Corrects speaker attribution leaks at turn edges using linguistic syntax cues.
⏳
2. Conversational Affective Inertia

Human emotional states have persistence. Transient acoustic blips do not mean true emotional shifts:

if duration ≤ 1.8s and curr_acoustic == ANGRY:
  if prev_turn == CALM and next_turn == CALM and text_is_calm:
    curr_acoustic = NEUTRAL // Inertia Smoothing
  • Eliminates false 1-second ANGRY spikes caused by coughing, sneezes, or receiver clicks.
  • Maintains high sensitivity to sustained agitation.
🎙️
3. 350ms Symmetric Context Padding

Slicing audio tightly at word boundaries creates high-frequency spectral clicks and cuts off voice attack formants. Slices fed into Emotion2Vec+ incorporate 350ms of ambient pre-roll and post-roll, boosting acoustic SER stability.

❓
4. Question-Intonation Normalization

Polite inquiries ("Would that still work for you?") feature rising pitch that acoustic models falsely flag as high-arousal Anger. When text is interrogative (ends with '?') and semantically neutral, the acoustic label is normalized to NEUTRAL.

Native Cross-Talk Modeling

Overlap Tracking: Who Interrupted Whom

Rather than forcing blind source separation or discarding overlap, our platform treats speech collisions as premier behavioral indicators.

⚡
The Interruption Detection Algorithm

Using Pyannote's diarize_with_overlaps(), intervals where multiple speakers are simultaneously active are identified.

For each speaker turn [S_start, S_end]:
  If OverlapInterval [O_start, O_end] intersects [S_start, S_end]:
    overlap_duration = min(S_end, O_end) - max(S_start, O_start)
    if S_start > other_speaker_start:
      is_interruption = TRUE
      interrupted_by = current_speaker
  • Attributed at both turn and session level.
  • Calculates global overtalk_ratio = overlap_sec / total_speech_sec.

Bimodal Emotion Fusion: Text vs. Tone Matrix

How AffectEvaluator detects emotional incongruence, hostility masking, and genuine satisfaction.

Semantic Sentiment (Qwen 72B) Acoustic Tone (Emotion2Vec+) Unified Classification Conversational Interpretation System Action
HAPPY ("Oh wonderful service") ANGRY / TENSE Hostile Sarcasm / Incongruence Customer is deeply dissatisfied but using mocking or sarcastic politeness. Flag Supervisor Urgent
ANGRY ("I can't believe this") HAPPY / LAUGHING Nervous Masking / Blended Affect Nervous laugh during bad news; customer is stressed rather than amused. Highlight Ambiguity
NEUTRAL ("Okay I will wait") SAD / TIRED Customer Resignation Passive surrender; customer is losing patience and prone to churn. Flag Retention Risk
HAPPY ("That resolved my issue") HAPPY / CALM Congruent Satisfaction Genuine positive resolution; text and tone agree with high confidence. Positive CSAT Log
🎯
Ambiguity Margin Detection

If the difference between the top-1 and top-2 emotion probabilities is $\le 0.15$ and the top score is below $0.65$, AffectEvaluator flags the utterance as is_ambiguous = True, preventing false overconfident classifications.
